The Know-Your-Agent Mandate Card Live

Free mini tool in this page, plus a Markdown card, prompts and team exercise · Added 20 November 2026 · Available until Sunday 29 November 2026, 11:59 PM IST

On 19 November, at the Singapore FinTech Festival, MAS managing director Chia Der Jiun said MAS would facilitate a “know your agent” protocol, so a bank can learn four things about an AI agent acting for a customer: who it is and whom it represents, what authority it has, what mandate it works under, and what assurance exists about where it came from and how reliable it is. He also said he would not advise giving personal agents the login details to your financial accounts (MAS keynote). A day earlier, AXS said it would open its network of more than 800 Singapore billers to AI agents, with Visa and Mastercard showing integrations and a Grab assistant trial planned, but no consumer launch date (The Straits Times).

The protocol hasn’t been published yet, but the four questions are already useful. This item turns them into a mandate card you write for any agent before it gets near your money: who it is, whom it acts for, look-only, prepare or pay-within-limits, a per-payment limit and monthly cap, how it connects (never your password), stop rules, how to switch it off, and a review date. The tool below builds the card in your browser and flags the gaps; nothing you type is sent or stored. The Markdown download adds a “would we accept this agent?” grid for bank and fintech teams, six prompts and a twenty-minute team exercise. Use only synthetic details, and your bank’s terms come first.

Why it expires: it is built around one week of news, before the protocol is published and before any consumer agent bill-payment service has a launch date. The four questions will last; this snapshot won’t. After Sunday 29 November the tool and card come down.
