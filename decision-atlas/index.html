06 / Reading this collection

Working frameworks, with their limits visible.

The collection draws on the Cloudera/panel technology doctrine, the September Decision Core and Nine Answers work, and the October AI autonomy discussions. Axis pairs and named concepts were recovered from working notes; quadrant guidance and plain-language measures have been edited for this demonstration.

Several pairs use explicit operational equivalents: impact as value, authority as autonomy, data confidence as trust, and evidence responsiveness as adaptability. The exact original 30-plus-word list and complete historical matrix inventory remain unverified. A named concept is not a claim of academic novelty or empirical validation.

Custom combinations and 3 × 3 extensions are exploratory. No single score establishes permission to act. Actual decisions require context, tested evidence, explicit authority and a named accountable owner.
