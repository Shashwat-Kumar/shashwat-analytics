Our editorial principles

  1. Evidence before enthusiasm. Report observed facts separately from interpretation, estimates and hypotheses.
  2. Sources before certainty. Attribute externally reported claims and link to original material where possible.
  3. Show the assumptions. Our tools explain their inputs, constraints and illustrative nature; a model output is not a decision mandate.
  4. Make corrections visible. We welcome error reports and correct substantiated mistakes, with an explanation where changes are material.
  5. Independence matters. Opinions and experiments here are not endorsed by employers, clients, publishers or featured organizations.

What we are — and aren't

ReadSigil is a publication, experiment space and learning project. It is not a DCB Bank property, a bank-approved advisory service or presented as a separately incorporated company.

Teaching cases use fictional examples or clearly identified public sources. Confidential employer or customer data does not belong here.
