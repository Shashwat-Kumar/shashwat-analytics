Scorecard cutoff

Credit scorecards trade approvals against bad debt. Move the cutoff, change how well the model separates good from bad, and see the trade-off.

Two normal score distributions, so Gini follows directly from separation. Stylised; see credit-risk-scorecard for a WoE/IV build on synthetic loans.

Brakes simulator

1,000 cases. The AI acts on its own above a confidence bar; the rest go to a person. Slide autonomy up and decide where the brakes belong.

Stylised: human review 40 units per case, a routine error 150, a high-stakes error 5,000. Illustrates Faster cars need better brakes. The full version, with stakes tiers, abstention, circuit breakers and 20,000 synthetic bank decisions, is the bounded-autonomy console.
