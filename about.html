About

Shashwat Kumar

Head, Business Intelligence Unit · DCB Bank · Mumbai

I have spent 15+ years leading analytics and AI across financial services, insurance and telecom/media: building data teams, KPI frameworks and customer models, and the governance that keeps them honest.

Today I head the Business Intelligence Unit at DCB Bank in Mumbai. My work spans decision intelligence, banking analytics and the governance required to put AI into real operations. Earlier, I headed advanced analytics and digital lending strategy at a retail lender (NBFC) and analytics at a health insurer, with previous roles in commercial planning and analytics across media, telecom and technology.

PGDM, XLRI Jamshedpur · PGPMI · Diploma in Advanced Analytics, Indian Statistical Institute · FRM Part I (GARP).

What I care about

Measurement that reflects real value rather than whatever is easiest to count. Automation designed with its brakes in mind. Data that is boring: consistent, documented and trusted. And simple answers, when the simple answer is good enough.

I still work hands-on in SQL and Python. The essays here are where I think out loud.

Code

Small open-source demos on synthetic data. They show methods only and contain no employer data.

  • bounded-autonomy: policy-as-code governance for AI decisions in banking, with abstention, circuit breakers, a tamper-evident ledger and a live console that re-runs 20,000 synthetic decisions in your browser
  • bank-analytics-agent: a tool-calling agent that turns a plain-English question into guarded, read-only SQL, then returns an answer and a chart
  • churn-propensity-model: gradient boosting against a logistic baseline, with SHAP and decile lift
  • credit-risk-scorecard: a WoE/IV PD scorecard with AUC, KS and calibration
