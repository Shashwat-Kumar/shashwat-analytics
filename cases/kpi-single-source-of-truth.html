01 / THE MANAGEMENT PROBLEM

More reporting had not created one trusted decision.

At fictional Northwind Retail, revenue, acquisition cost and repeat-purchase rate appeared in separate management views. The stated demand was for a consolidated KPI dashboard. That is a solution request, not yet a problem definition.

The real decision: should leadership invest in customer acquisition, retention or neither until the economics are reconciled? A dashboard that presents conflicting denominators faster would accelerate the wrong decision.

Business sponsor

Commercial / finance leadership (proposed role for this exercise).

Decision frequency

Monthly resource allocation, supported by weekly exceptions.

Base population

Illustrative 12 months, October 2025 to September 2026.

Decision constraint

No validated contribution margin, paid-channel attribution or cohort profitability in the source sample.

Three hypotheses to investigate

  1. Volume effect: transaction count, rather than basket size, explains the endpoint revenue change mechanically.
  2. Acquisition-quality effect: increased CAC could still be rational if retained contribution per acquired customer improved; LTV and channel mix are missing.
  3. Definition effect: repeat rate may use a different customer cohort or lookback window than revenue. Alignment is necessary before attribution.

04 / RECOMMENDED INTERVENTION

Build a governed metric layer, then a decision pack.

  1. STEP 01Contract definitions. Revenue net of returns? Paid orders or placed orders? CAC as channel spend divided by new paying customers? Repeat within what cohort and period? Agree and version each definition.
  2. STEP 02Reconcile source systems. Daily order, payment, return and advertising aggregates feed a reconciled table. All exceptions have owners and a documented waiver path.
  3. STEP 03Expose trade-offs. Revenue growth, CAC, contribution after variable cost, repeat behavior, channel mix and customer cohorts sit together with provenance.
  4. STEP 04Establish the forum. Finance signs off on margin; commercial leaders assign actions; BI owns lineage, freshness and unresolved breaks—not the business decision.
Operating principle: a single source of truth is a shared definition plus reconciliation process, not simply one database or one preferred report.

05 / VALUE & CONTROL

The dashboard is not the outcome.

Proposed acceptance thresholds below are design targets for a hypothetical pilot. They are not measured results for Northwind.

Value equation: incremental contribution = incremental net sales × contribution margin − incremental acquisition spend − incremental operating expense. A richer-looking dashboard gets no credit by itself.

06 / DELIVERY PLAN

A 90-day roadmap with actual decision owners.

  1. DAYS 01–30Finance and Commercial freeze KPI contracts; Data/BI documents source lineage and establishes the discrepancy baseline.
  2. DAYS 31–60Data Engineering creates reconciled tables and tests with at least three conflicting legacy reports. Owners sign off on exceptions.
  3. DAYS 61–90Commercial runs the decision pack with channel/cohort economics; independent finance review validates contribution and meeting actions.

Stop rule: do not approve scaled media reallocation based on this dashboard while CAC attribution, customer cohorts or variable contribution remain unreconciled.
