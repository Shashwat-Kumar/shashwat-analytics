Is the clever model worth it? A churn case

Contoso Play · Subscription video

A gradient-boosted churn model set honestly against a simple baseline. The gain is small (AUC 0.80 against 0.79), which is itself the finding: the value lies less in the score than in understanding the drivers (SHAP) and in where to act (gains curve).

Open-source code

Small open-source demos on synthetic data. They show methods only and contain no employer data.

  • bounded-autonomy: policy-as-code governance for AI decisions in banking, with abstention, circuit breakers, a tamper-evident ledger and a live console that re-runs 20,000 synthetic decisions in your browser
  • bank-analytics-agent: a tool-calling agent that turns a plain-English question into guarded, read-only SQL, then returns an answer and a chart
  • churn-propensity-model: gradient boosting against a logistic baseline, with SHAP and decile lift
  • credit-risk-scorecard: a WoE/IV PD scorecard with AUC, KS and calibration
