Intelligence without a main character

Much of the conversation about general AI imagines a single brilliant system that does everything. I am not sure that is how it will show up.

No individual ever built a city, a hospital system or a body of science alone. What made people collectively capable was division of labour, shared tools, written memory and institutions that let strangers coordinate. Intelligence at civilisational scale has always been a team sport.

Software is heading the same way. Specialised agents that search, write, check, plan and call tools are already being chained together, and the interesting behaviour emerges from how they hand work to one another rather than from any one of them. That shifts the hard questions. Who sets the shared goal? How do agents verify each other's work? What happens when one of them steps outside its sandbox?

For anyone building with AI inside an organisation, I think this is the more useful frame. Stop waiting for the one model that does it all. Design the orchestration, the interfaces and the checks between components, because that is where both the capability and the risk will live.
