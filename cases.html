II / Publicly documented implementations

What real cases are willing to measure.

Six curated examples from consulting and technology publishers. Results are source-reported, not independently audited by ReadSigil.

McKinseyInternal transformation

Rewiring knowledge work with Lilli

72% activeMcKinsey colleagues using Lilli after firmwide rollout

Challenge. Knowledge was spread across the firm, slowing search and synthesis.

Intervention. Build a secure internal generative-AI knowledge platform and change how teams work with knowledge.

Published evidence. McKinsey reports 72% active use and colleagues reporting up to 30% time savings on searching and synthesizing information.

What to learn. Adoption and workflow redesign belong in the result; an impressive demo is insufficient.

Read critically. Adoption and self-reported time savings are not an independent causal productivity estimate.

BCGEnterprise transformation

IBM: productivity translated into economics

$4.5B+reported bottom-line impact delivered

Challenge. Complex enterprise operations consumed capacity that could be redirected to more valuable work.

Intervention. Reimagine workflows across functions, simplify operations and connect AI-enabled productivity to business results.

Published evidence. BCG reports more than $4.5 billion of bottom-line impact delivered and over $6 billion in identified value potential.

What to learn. Distinguish delivered impact from a pipeline of opportunities, and report both separately.

Read critically. The measures are claims in BCG's client-impact publication; identified value is not realized value.

BainBusiness transformation

ApplianceCo: ambition versus realization

40% projectedenterprise-value increase over three years, not an achieved return

Challenge. Stagnating sales and aging ways of working made the existing business model less competitive.

Intervention. Co-create a digital transformation strategy spanning consumer experience, workflow redesign and efficiency.

Published evidence. Bain describes 12+ digital initiatives and a projected 40% enterprise-value improvement over three years.

What to learn. Put expected value, delivery milestones and realized value in separate columns.

Read critically. Bain's 40% figure is a forward-looking expectation, not a verified outcome.

Google CloudBanking data and AI

Monzo: an analytics foundation that serves decisions

Minutesto analyze up-to-date multi-terabyte datasets via Looker

Challenge. A rapidly scaling digital bank needed consistent data for operations, product and risk decisions.

Intervention. Use BigQuery and Looker to connect data modeling with analytics and machine-learning workflows.

Published evidence. Google Cloud describes nearly 19 PB in BigQuery, 2,000+ data models and multi-terabyte analysis in minutes.

What to learn. Data access matters when it changes the reach and timeliness of decisions.

Read critically. Platform scale and query speed are operating metrics; they are not a quantified profit or customer-outcome gain.

AWSBranch analytics

Together Credit Union: insight arrives before the month closes

10–15 secondsreported query times, versus 15–20 minutes previously

Challenge. Branch managers waited for reporting that could be nearly two months old.

Intervention. Create a centralized data lake and self-service branch dashboards using Amazon Redshift.

Published evidence. The AWS case reports query performance changing from 15–20 minutes to 10–15 seconds, plus near-real-time branch visibility.

What to learn. The useful KPI is decision latency, not the existence of another dashboard.

Read critically. The performance comparison is workload-specific and does not itself quantify incremental branch growth.

Read the original AWS case ↗
Google CloudCredit operations

Lloyds: reducing a mortgage process bottleneck

Days → secondsreported time for the income-verification step

Challenge. Complex data and machine-learning workflows constrained the mortgage experience and innovation speed.

Intervention. Modernize machine-learning infrastructure and automate a specific income-verification task.

Published evidence. Google Cloud reports that the income-verification step in mortgage applications fell from days to seconds.

What to learn. Name the precise process stage that improved; do not suggest the whole loan journey now takes seconds.

Read critically. This is a vendor-published metric for one verification step, not an end-to-end mortgage approval SLA.

III / Case-study standard

A case is not four bullet points and an animation.

Good case writing lets a skeptical reader reconstruct the management decision, assess the evidence and distinguish a credible outcome from a convenient story.

That is the standard these pages now use.

View underlying work samples →Build a working prototype →
  1. Decision to be made. A named business problem, scope, owner and trade-off.
  2. Baseline and denominator. Where the organization started, when and how the measure was defined.
  3. Diagnostic reasoning. Hypotheses, segment differences, drivers and alternative explanations.
  4. Intervention and choices. What was changed, what could have been done instead, and what depended on what.
  5. Outcome and attribution. Results with their evidence standard: realized, reported, estimated, projected or synthetic.
  6. Boundaries. Missing evidence, causal uncertainty, risks and the next experiment or management decision.
