REFERENCE ROUTE

What this does and does not establish

These are synthetic cases with outcomes written in their author's expectations and calculated by the frozen Python kernel during development. An agreement does not establish a correct real-world decision. Economic assumptions, feasible choices and approval rights still require independent evidence. All results are provisional and subject to review.

02 / THE ROUTING RULE

Five checks. One proposed next action.

Why investigate further, and when is it time to act? See how five questions guide the next step.

01 / DUTY

Can we act lawfully?

Identify binding requirements, the authority holder and whether an authorised option can meet the deadline.

02 / OVERDUE

What expired?

Record overdue evidence and approved fallback. Missing approval triggers escalation—not implied consent.

03 / OBJECTIVE

What are we optimising?

When competing objectives imply different choices, return the decision to the accountable owner.

04 / MATERIALITY

Could evidence matter?

Screen all plausible uncertainties. A possible upside is an upper bound, never proof an investigation pays.

05 / EVIDENCE

Will a real test pay?

Compare specific test accuracy, cost, delay and feasibility. Stop when no justified timely study has net value.

OUTPUT / HUMAN ACCOUNTABILITY

Record the reason.

State the next action, evidence requested, owner, cost and deadline. Humans retain approval and legal responsibility.
