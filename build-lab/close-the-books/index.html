Where do you start?

Break register

    Correct the books

    Data Platform cannot post to the core ledger. Each correction goes to Finance Control for approval, then the correction job posts what was approved. One break is on the clock: under RBI's turnaround-time rules, a beneficiary bank has until T+1, which is today, to credit the customer or return the money. After that it owes the customer ₹100 a day.
