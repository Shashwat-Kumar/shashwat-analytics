READSIGIL / PROMPT LAB

Let a model fix your prompt.

Paste a prompt template, a few test cases and what “good” means. Prompt Lab runs it, scores every answer, asks a stronger model why the weak ones failed, rewrites the prompt and repeats until the scores stop improving.

Runs entirely in this tab. Your key goes only to the provider you choose; nothing is sent to ReadSigil. Text inside <protect>…</protect> is never rewritten. Open source: Shashwat-Kumar/promptlab.

1 · Setup

Off by default. If ticked, the key is kept in this browser tab until you close it. Use a low-limit or free-tier key, and do not tick this on a shared or public computer.

2 · Rounds

Press Optimize, or try the demo.
