Results-Season AI Claims Decoder Live

Free prompt pack and scorecard (Markdown) · Added 23 October 2026 · Available until Sunday 1 November 2026, 11:59 PM IST

Listed lenders and fintechs are publishing their July–September quarter results, and the investor decks are full of lines about AI and GenAI. This pack helps you tell a measured outcome from a press-release adjective: six prompts that pull out every AI claim verbatim, sort it from “number with a baseline” down to “adjective only”, write the follow-up question that would make it checkable, and compare it with last quarter.

It ends with a ten-question scorecard for comparing documents. It is not about any particular company, it works only on public documents, and every figure a model quotes should be checked against the source. It is a reading aid, not investment advice.

Why it expires: it is most useful while results documents are fresh and calls are still happening. After 1 November the pack comes down.

Festive-Season Scam Watch prompt pack Live

Free prompt pack (Markdown) · Added 22 October 2026 · Available until Friday 30 October 2026, 11:59 PM IST

Eight prompts for people who write customer communication or review fraud and complaint queues ahead of the festive shopping peak. They help you draft SMS-length scam alerts, check translations by back-translating them, group synthetic complaints into likely scam patterns, red-team your own customer messages, and write a calm call-centre script for a customer who may have shared an OTP.

The pack is built around one rule: never paste real customer data into a public AI tool. Every prompt works on synthetic or redacted examples, and every output is a first draft for your own fraud, compliance and brand teams to own.

Why it expires: these prompts are most useful in the weeks before the festive rush, while there is still time to brief staff and send alerts. After 30 October the window has largely passed, so the pack comes down.
