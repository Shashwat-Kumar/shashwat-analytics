Is the clever model worth it? A churn exploration

“Contoso Play” · fictional

A gradient-boosted churn model set honestly against a simple baseline. On this synthetic data the gain is small (AUC 0.80 against 0.79), which is itself the finding: the value lies less in the score than in understanding the drivers (SHAP) and in where to act (gains curve).

Readiness as a map: a data-protection exploration

“Acme Learning” · fictional

What does readiness for India's DPDP Act look like for a mid-sized organisation? This exploration scores each processing activity, surfaces the gaps (in this sample, 50% overall and 23 gaps) and works a roadmap back from the date core obligations apply. Not legal advice.

Small open-source demos on synthetic data. They show methods only and contain no employer data.
