How the simulation works

Every week new items enter the review queue. Verification processes up to its capacity. A chosen percentage returns for rework; the remainder enters the release queue. Release clears up to its own capacity. Backlogs carry into the next week. We use fractional expected-value flows, rounded only for display, rather than random outcomes.

There is no queue abandonment, changing complexity, different risk classes or model-learning effect. It is a constraint illustration, not a full queueing model.

What the real research says

Demirer, Musolff and Yang's September 2026 NBER revision estimates a 240% cumulative rise in coding activity (commits) alongside 30% more shipped releases from advanced AI tool adoption. That is empirical evidence from software developers; the percentages in this lab are independently chosen teaching inputs.
